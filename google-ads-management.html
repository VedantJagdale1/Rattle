The Problem

Where ad budgets actually go

When we audit an underperforming Google Ads account, it is almost always the same five things. Broad match keywords with no negative list, so you pay for searches that were never going to buy. No conversion tracking, so nobody can tell which half of the budget works.

Ads pointing at a homepage instead of a page about the thing that was searched for. A single campaign mixing brand and non-brand terms, making the whole account look profitable because people searching your own name were always going to convert. And an agency taking a percentage of spend, which quietly rewards them for spending more of your money.

Fixing those five things is usually worth more than any amount of new creative.

What You Get

What Google Ads management includes

Built, tracked and optimised in-house. You own the account throughout.

  • Account audit and restructureWasted spend identified first: broad match without negatives, irrelevant placements, overlapping campaigns, and brand terms inflating the numbers. Often the single biggest win available.
  • Campaign build across the right channelsSearch, Performance Max, Google Shopping for e-commerce, Display and YouTube remarketing, plus Meta and LinkedIn where your buyers actually are. We recommend the mix rather than selling all of them.
  • Conversion tracking done properlyGA4, Google Ads conversions, call tracking and form attribution configured and tested. Without this everything else is guesswork — and it is missing in most accounts we inherit.
  • Landing pages built to convertWe build the page the ad points at, not just the ad. Sending paid traffic to a homepage is the most common and most expensive mistake in Indian PPC.
  • Keyword and negative managementSearch-term reports reviewed weekly, with negatives added continuously. This is unglamorous and it is where most of the savings come from.
  • Weekly optimisationBids, budgets, ad copy tests and audience adjustments — reviewed every week, not left on autopilot between monthly reports.
  • Monthly reporting on cost per leadNot impressions, not clicks, not “engagement”. What each enquiry cost you and how that trend is moving.
How It Works

How a campaign gets launched

Most accounts are judged too early. Here is the honest sequence.

01

Free account review

If you already run ads, we audit the account and show you where the waste is. If you do not, we size the opportunity and estimate a realistic budget.

02

Tracking first

Conversion tracking is built and verified before a rupee is spent. Launching without it is how agencies hide poor performance.

03

Structure and build

Campaigns, ad groups, keywords, negatives and ad copy built around how your buyers search — with brand and non-brand separated so the reporting stays honest.

04

Landing pages

We build or fix the destination page so the click has somewhere useful to land.

05

Learning period

Weeks one to six the account is calibrating. We optimise weekly but resist wholesale changes, because judging a campaign in week one is how good accounts get throttled before they work.

06

Scale what converts

Once cost per lead stabilises, budget shifts toward the campaigns and keywords producing real enquiries, and away from everything else.

Straight Talk

When this isn't right for you

Your budget is too small for your market. In competitive categories a click can cost more than a hundred rupees, and a budget that generates two clicks a day cannot produce statistically meaningful data, let alone consistent leads. We will tell you before you spend if your budget will not clear that bar — and suggest where it would work harder instead.

You want to judge it in week one. The first four to six weeks are a learning period where the account is buying data as much as leads. If the plan is to switch it off after ten days of unimpressive numbers, the money is better kept in your pocket.

Nobody follows up on the leads. Paid search produces enquiries that go cold within hours. If there is no one to call them back the same day, ads will look like they failed when the real problem is downstream.
