The Problem

What makes a business website fail

Most underperforming sites are not ugly. They are slow, unclear, and built for a desktop that almost nobody uses to find you.

A homepage carousel that takes six seconds to load on 4G. No phone number visible without scrolling. A contact form that silently fails. Copy that describes the company instead of answering the visitor's question. And a build so locked down that changing a price means emailing an agency and waiting three days.

We fix the boring things first, because the boring things are what cost you enquiries.

What You Get

What a website project includes

Fixed scope, fixed timeline, fixed price — agreed in writing before we start.

  • Discovery and structureWho the site is for, what they need to find, and what you want them to do. We agree the sitemap and page structure before any design happens.
  • Design that matches your brandMobile-first layouts designed for the phone and scaled up, not desktop designs squeezed down. You see and approve designs before development begins.
  • The right platform for youWordPress when you need to edit content easily, Shopify or WooCommerce for stores, Next.js or custom code when you need something a template cannot do. We recommend based on your situation, not on what we prefer to build.
  • Speed and Core Web VitalsImage optimisation, lazy loading, minimal scripts and proper caching — tested on a mid-range Android phone on a real connection, not just on a fast laptop.
  • SEO foundationsClean semantic markup, title and meta structure, schema, XML sitemap, robots and analytics — handled during the build rather than retrofitted later at extra cost.
  • Integrations that matter in IndiaRazorpay or Stripe payments, WhatsApp click-to-chat, Google Business Profile, CRM and lead routing, and email or newsletter tools.
  • Training and handoverA CMS your team can actually use, a walkthrough session, and written documentation. Plus hosting setup, SSL, backups and a post-launch support window.
How It Works

How a build runs

Staged, with your approval at each gate — so there are no surprises at the end.

01

Scope and quote

We agree exactly what is being built, what it costs and when it lands. Written down, so nothing is ambiguous later.

02

Structure and content

Sitemap, page-by-page outline and copy. Content is the usual bottleneck, so we start it early rather than at the end.

03

Design

Mobile and desktop layouts for key pages, reviewed and approved before a line of code is written.

04

Build

Development in stages you can see on a staging link, so feedback arrives while it is still cheap to act on.

05

Test

Real devices, real connection speeds, every form submitted, every link checked, accessibility and speed verified.

06

Launch and handover

Go live, tracking confirmed working, training session delivered, and the repository and accounts transferred to you.

Straight Talk

When this isn't right for you

You need it live next week. A brochure site can be turned around quickly, but anything with e-commerce, custom functionality or real content work cannot be compressed without something being skipped — usually testing or speed. We would rather decline than ship something that embarrasses both of us.

The content is not ready and nobody will write it. This is the single most common reason website projects stall for months. We can write it for you as part of the scope, but somebody has to, and it needs to be agreed up front rather than discovered halfway through.

You want the cheapest possible build. There is always someone cheaper. What you usually get is a page-builder site with twenty plugins that loads in nine seconds and cannot be maintained. If price is the only criterion, we are not the right fit.
