The Problem

Why most SEO spend is wasted

Most businesses we audit are paying for activity, not outcomes. A monthly PDF of rankings for keywords nobody searches. Blog posts written for a word count. Backlinks bought in bulk from sites Google stopped trusting years ago.

Meanwhile the actual problems sit untouched: pages that take nine seconds to load on 4G, product pages with no unique text, a Google Business Profile that has not been opened since it was created, and no tracking that connects an enquiry back to the search that produced it.

We start by finding out which of those is costing you the most, then fix that first.

What You Get

What an SEO engagement actually includes

Every item below is delivered and reported monthly. No line item you cannot see the output of.

  • Technical SEO audit and fixesCrawlability, indexing, site speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, schema markup, redirect chains and broken links — audited up front, then monitored continuously.
  • Keyword and competitor researchThe terms your buyers actually type, mapped to a specific page on your site, with search volume and difficulty stated plainly so you can see which are realistic this year.
  • On-page optimisationTitles, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking and content depth on the pages that matter — starting with pages already ranking on page two, because those move fastest.
  • Local SEO and Google Business ProfileProfile optimisation, category and service setup, citation consistency across directories, and a review generation process. For a business serving a city, this is the highest return per hour in all of SEO.
  • Content that targets real queriesArticles and landing pages written to answer a specific search, not to hit a word count. You approve the topics before anything is written.
  • Authority and link buildingDigital PR, genuine guest placements and citation cleanup. We do not buy links in bulk — that is how sites get penalised.
  • Monthly report and callRankings against your tracked keyword set, organic traffic, conversions, what we did, and what changes next month. In plain English, with a call to walk through it.

Straight Talk

When this isn't right for you

You need leads in the next 30 days. SEO will not do that. Nothing honest will. If your pipeline is empty right now, put the budget into Google Ads first — it produces enquiries in week one — and add SEO once there is breathing room.

You cannot commit for at least six months. Most SEO campaigns that "did not work" simply stopped in month three, right before the compounding starts. If a six-month commitment is not realistic for your cash flow, we would rather tell you now than take three months of fees and leave you with nothing.

You want a guarantee of #1 rankings. Google does not sell positions, and any agency guaranteeing them is either targeting keywords with no search volume or using tactics that will get your site penalised. We will not compete on that promise.
