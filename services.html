Case Study

ERP system for an optical lens manufacturer

Rattle's founder led development as the largest single contributor on a seven-person engineering team.

The problem. Lens labs run on a chain of disconnected spreadsheets — sales orders in one, stock in another, dispatch and quality control on paper, retailer balances reconciled by hand at month end. Errors surface weeks late, usually as money owed that nobody can account for.

What was built. A single ERP covering the whole operation: accounting and ledgers, CRM, inventory with bill-of-materials, purchase orders and goods receipt, dispatch with live delivery tracking, quality control, tiered pricing and dealer schemes, HR, onboarding and verification — served to four connected clients from one API.

The hard part. Field executives work where there is no signal. The mobile app buffers work offline and batch-syncs when connectivity returns, so every balance has to reconcile from the ledger rather than from whichever device wrote last. Making a retailer's outstanding dues agree across the executive's phone, the web ERP and the printed invoice was the real engineering problem.

Straight Talk

What we don't promise

Most agency pitches are a list of guarantees. Here is the shorter, more useful list — the things we refuse to promise, and why. If another agency promises you any of these, ask them how.

We don't guarantee #1 rankings

Google doesn't sell positions and no agency controls its algorithm. A guarantee of position one usually means either keywords nobody searches, or tactics that get sites penalised. We commit to the work and to reporting the rankings honestly, up or down.

We don't guarantee a lead count

Lead volume depends on your pricing, your market and how fast your team follows up — not just on campaigns. We commit to the campaigns, the tracking and the optimisation, and we show you the cost per lead every month so you can judge it yourself.

We don't promise overnight SEO

Local visibility moves in weeks; competitive organic rankings take months. We'd rather lose the pitch than sell you a 30-day miracle and spend month two explaining why it didn't happen.

We don't take a cut of your ad spend

Percentage-of-spend pricing rewards an agency for spending more of your money, not for earning you more. We charge a flat management fee, so cutting wasted spend costs us nothing.

We don't hold your accounts hostage

Your domain, website, Google Ads, Analytics and Business Profile are created in your name from day one. Thirty days' notice and you walk away with all of it, plus the source code.

We don't sell a channel that won't work

If your budget is too thin for the keywords you want, or your customers aren't on the platform you're asking about, we'll tell you before you spend — even when that means a smaller invoice for us.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us your goal and budget. We'll tell you honestly which channel will move the needle first — even if it isn't the one you came in asking about.
