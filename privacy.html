Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us — in plain language.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Who we are

Rattle is a digital marketing and technology agency based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, working with clients across the country. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Rattle. "You" means anyone visiting this website or contacting us through it.

For anything in this policy, write to rattledeveloper@gmail.com or call +91 80102 18846.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our enquiry forms. Nothing on this website tracks you, profiles you, or collects information in the background.

  • Required: your name, phone number, email address, the service you need, and your message.
  • Optional: your company name, website address and budget range, if you choose to share them.
  • Automatic: which page of our site your enquiry came from, so we know what you were reading.

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, payment card details, bank details, passwords, Aadhaar or PAN numbers through this website.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry — to call or email you back, prepare the free audit you asked for, and discuss working together. We do not use your details for automated decision-making or profiling.

Who else sees it

Our enquiry forms are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-forwarding service that passes your submission to our email inbox. Your message therefore passes through FormSubmit's systems and arrives in our email account, which is hosted by Google (Gmail). Both process your data under their own privacy policies.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with any other third party unless the law requires us to.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. We load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them; Google may log your IP address as part of serving that request.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this page before doing so.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our email inbox so we have a record of our conversation. If you become a client, we keep your contact details for as long as we work together and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet tax and accounting obligations. If you don't become a client, ask us to delete your enquiry and we will.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information of yours we hold
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete it, where we have no legal reason to keep it
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you are unable to

Email rattledeveloper@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, so what you type is encrypted in transit. We keep access to our email inbox limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Children

This website is meant for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be explained here rather than made quietly.
